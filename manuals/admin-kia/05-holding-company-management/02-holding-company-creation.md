## Holding Company Creation

![Creating Holding Company](images/creating-holding-company.png){width=5.33in}

- This screen is for registering a new holding company.

**Holding company information**

- **Company name**

  - Required field.

  - Maximum 50 characters.

  - Duplicate names are not allowed.

- **Name of Company Representative**

  - Optional field.

  - Maximum 20 characters.

**Affiliate Dealer List**

- A dealer can belong to only one holding company.

- The Available Dealer list shows dealers that are not currently assigned to any holding company.

- Each dealer entry shows: name, dealer number, and status.

  - Dealers are displayed in the following status priority: Active, Under Termination, Inactive, Terminated then sorted alphabetically.

- You can search by dealer name within both the Available Dealer list and Selected dealers.

- When a dealer is selected from the Available Dealer list, it will appear in the Selected dealers section.

  - Unchecking a dealer or clicking the x button removes them from the Selected dealers list.

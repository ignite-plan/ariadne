## Zone/District Management View

![Zone/District List](images/zone-district-list.png){width=6.26in}

- You can view the Zone/District to which the dealer is assigned.

- You can create, edit, and delete Zones and Districts.

![Adding Zone](images/adding-zone.png){width=4.29in}

- All created Zones and Districts can be viewed.

- The number of dealers assigned to each Zone and District is displayed in parentheses next to the name.

- Use the +/- button to expand or collapse the structure.

- Clicking on a Zone or District will display the list of dealers assigned to the selected area on the right panel.

  - **ALL**: Displays a list of all created dealers.

  - **Unassigned**: Dealers that are either not assigned to a Zone, or belong to a Zone but not assigned to any District.

  - Dealer list is sorted in order of most recently assigned.

  - **No**: The sequence number of the dealer list.

  - **Zone**: The name of the assigned Zone.

  - **District**: The name of the assigned District.

  - **Dealer no.**: The dealer's identification number.

  - **Dealer name**: The name of the dealer.

  - **Status**: The current status of the dealer.

  - **Updated date**: The date when the Zone/District information of the dealer was last updated.

  - **Detail**: Provides a detailed view of the dealer.

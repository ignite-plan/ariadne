## Bulletin Summary List View

- **Total Count**: Displays the total number of **Bulletin Summaries**.

- **Bulletin Summary Subject**:\
  Displays the **subject** of each summary.\
  You can distinguish whether the summary is **Weekly** or **Target-based**, and identify the **posting period** it covers.\
  Clicking the subject opens the **Bulletin Summary Email** pop-up, where the summary content can be viewed in **English (EN)** and **French (FR)**.

- **Target Posting Period (EST)**:\
  Shows the date range that the bulletin summary covers.

- **Created Date (EST)**:\
  Displays the time the bulletin summary was generated.

- **Email**:\
  Clicking the **\[Send\]** button opens the **Bulletin Summary Email** pop-up, where the summary content can be viewed in **English (EN)** and **French (FR)**.

## Job List View

![Job List](images/job-list.png){width=6.26in}

This screen allows you to view all created **Jobs**.

- **Job Code**: A unique code automatically assigned when a job is created.

- **Job Title**: The name of the job.

- **Description**: A brief explanation of the job\'s role or purpose.

- **Updated Date**: The ID of the last user who updated the job.

- **Status**: Indicates whether the job is active or inactive.

  - **Active**: The job is active and will appear as an available option in the **Job selection** dropdown on the **Dealer Employee Management** screen.

  - **Inactive**: The job is inactive and will **not** appear as a selectable option in the **Dealer Employee Management** screen.

- Dealer employees who were already assigned to an inactive job can still **log in**, but **cannot use the service**.

![Job List - Search](images/job-list-search.png){width=6.26in}

**Job Search Filters**

The Job List screen provides search filters to help you find specific jobs.

- Job Title: Search by job title.

- Job Code: Search by job code.

- Status: Filter by job status (Active/Inactive).

- Reset: Clears all selected filter conditions.

- Search: Performs a search based on the selected filters.

\[Create New\]: Click to navigate to the Job Creation screen.

- This is a screen where you can **manage dealer employees** who belong to the same dealer company as the user.

- You can view the information of dealer employees. Depending on your **permissions**, you can create new employees or modify employee information.

- You can access this screen through dealer employee management in the upper right. The dealer employee management menu name is displayed to users

- Only users with the **Dealer Principal** or **Super Admin** job roles can access and view this screen.

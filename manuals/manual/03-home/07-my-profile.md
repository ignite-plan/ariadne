## My Profile

- This screen shows your personal information and the business information registered for the dealership you are currently signed in to.

- You can view your profile and edit your personal information.

- To open it, click the profile icon in the GNB to open the account side sheet, then click **\[My Profile\]**.



![My Profile](images/my-profile.png){width=6.27in}


**Profile Summary**

- **ID**: Your account ID, managed through hmg-PartnerID. A tooltip explains that hmg-PartnerID is a unified account credential system provided by Hyundai Motor Group to business partners and suppliers.

- **Picture**: Your registered photo.

  - If no photo is registered, an initial avatar is displayed.

  - For Latin names, the avatar shows the first letter of the first name and the last name (for example, Isabel Marant → IM).

  - For non-Latin names, the avatar shows only the first letter of the last name.

- **Name**: Shown in the order First name, Last name.

- **Account email**: Your registered account email.

- A badge is displayed if you are the dealer principal.

**My profile**

- **ID**: Your account ID.

- **Personal phone**: Your personal phone number.

- **Language**: Your language setting.

- **Age**: Calculated from your date of birth (international age). If no date of birth is registered, "-" is displayed.

**Business Profile**

- This information is registered by your dealership administrator and cannot be edited by you.

- **Dealership**: The dealership you are currently signed in to.

- **Business email**: The business email registered for the current dealership.

- **Business phone**: The business phone number registered for the current dealership.


**Edit My Profile**

- Click **\[Edit\]** to switch to the **Edit My Profile** screen.



![Edit My Profile](images/edit-my-profile.png){width=6.27in}



- **Picture**

  - Click the picture area to upload an image file.

  - Supported image formats: JPEG (JPG) or PNG, up to 20MB in size.

  - If a picture is already registered, click the x button to delete it. The profile is saved without a picture.

  - Required (marked with \*) if you are the dealer principal of the dealership.

  - The picture change is applied when you click **\[Save\]**.

- **First Name**

  - Required. Max 20 characters.

- **Last Name**

  - Required. Max 20 characters.

- **ID**

  - Read-only.

- **Account Email**

  - Required. Max 50 characters.

  - Help text: Enter your personal email address. This is used for account management and password reset.

  - The email format is checked as you type. If the format is invalid, "Please enter a valid email" is displayed below the field.

- **Personal Phone**

  - Optional.

  - Select a country code (+1 or +82). +1 is selected by default.

  - Up to 15 digits. Only numbers and "-" are allowed.

- **Language**

  - Required. Select English or French.

  - If the dealership supports only one language, the field is shown as a read-only text field and cannot be changed.

- **Date of Birth**

  - Optional.

  - Placeholder: MM-DD-YYYY. A date picker opens when you click the field.

- **Business Profile**

  - Read-only. Guide text: This information is registered by your dealership administrator.


**Buttons**

- Click **\[Save\]** to save your changes.

  - After saving, the changed information is displayed. If you changed the language, the new language is applied as your default.

  - A notification email about the update is sent to your account email. The email lists only the titles of the changed items, not the details.

- Click **\[Cancel\]** to cancel editing.




**Dealer Information**

- This screen shows the information of the dealership you are currently signed in to. It is read-only and has no buttons or input fields.

- To open it, click the arrow (>) next to the dealership name on Home.

- The screen title is **Dealer Information**.



![Company Information](images/company-information.png){width=6.27in}



**Dealership Summary (left area)**

- **Company Name**: The name of the dealership.

- **Dealer No.**: The dealer number of the dealership.

**Company Overview**

- The information is created in Dealer Management. If there is no information, "-" is displayed.

- **Company name**: The dealership name.

- **Email**: The email registered for the dealership.

- **Website**: The dealership website.

- **Phone number**: The dealership phone number.

- **Address**: The dealership address.

- **Zip/Postal code**: The dealership zip or postal code.

- **Founding Date**: The date the dealership was founded.

- **Total Facilities**: The total number of facilities the dealership has.

- **Facility Type (Registered)**: The registered facility types and the number registered for each, in the form {facility type}(n).


**Dealer Principal Information**

- **First Name**: The first name of the dealer principal.

- **Last Name**: The last name of the dealer principal.

- **Email**: The business email of the dealer principal.

- **Business Phone**: The business phone number of the dealer principal.

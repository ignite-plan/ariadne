## Consent Notification

- In the case of customer **consent notifications**, these are used when customer consent is needed due to policy changes, etc. ![](images/consent-notification-img-1.png){width=4.45in}

Consent notification

- By clicking the **button**, you can agree or refuse terms and conditions, and navigate to the terms screen.

- In case there is **only one button**, the button name is provided as \"**Accepted**\".

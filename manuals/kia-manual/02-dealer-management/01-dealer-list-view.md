## Dealer List View

- This screen provides a list of all registered dealers.

- You can create a new dealer, or view and edit details of existing dealers.

![Dealer List](images/dealer-list.png){width=6.26in}

**Dealer Information Columns**

- **Company name**: The official dealer company name registered in HMG Partner.

- **Dealer No.**: Dealer Number managed within the Dealers system.

- **Dealer name**: The name of the dealer company.

- **Zone**: Indicates the dealer's assigned zone. If no zone is assigned, it will be displayed as "Unassigned."

- **District**: Indicates the dealer's assigned district. If no district is assigned, it will be displayed as "Unassigned."

- **Business end date**: The latest business end date among all business info associated with the dealer.

- **Updated by**: The ID of the user who created or last modified the dealer information.

- **Updated date**: The date the dealer was created or last modified.

- **Status**: Current status of the dealer.

  - **Draft**: Dealer creation in progress. Information is not yet finalized.

  - **Active**: Dealer is active and allowed to use services.

  - **Inactive**: Dealer is inactive and not allowed to use services.

  - **Under termination**: Dealer contract has expired, temporarily accessible.

  - **Terminated**: Dealer contract has expired and temporary access is also ended. The status automatically changes after 180 days from the Under termination status. Dealer employees belonging to the dealer cannot use the service.

- Click the **Location icon** to open a popup showing the registered address of the dealer.

![Location Popup](images/location-popup.png){width=3.41in}

- **Detail**: Opens the detailed view of the dealer.

- If the dealer status is **Draft**, two additional icons are available:

  - ![](images/dealer-list-view-img-1.png){width=0.24in} :Continue the dealer creation process (navigates to the edit screen).

  - ![](images/dealer-list-view-img-2.png){width=0.24in}:Delete the draft dealer. Deleted draft information cannot be restored.

- Click the **\[create\]** button at the top of the page to register a new dealer.

**Dealer Search & Filters**

- You can search for dealers by specific criteria.

![Dealer List - Search](images/dealer-list-search.png){width=6.26in}

- Choose one of the following fields to perform a keyword search:

  - **Dealer No**: Dealer number assigned by the Dealers system

  - **Dealer name**: Registered name of the dealer

  - **Company no.**: Dealer company number registered in HMG Partner

  - **Company name**: Official company name registered in HMG Partner

- You can filter dealers by **Business end date**:

  - View dealers whose business end date is within 60 or 90 days from today.

- You can filter by **Status** of the dealer.

- Click **\[Search\]** to apply the selected filters and view results.

- Click **\[Reset\]** to clear all filter conditions.

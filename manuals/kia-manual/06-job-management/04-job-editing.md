## Job Editing

- From the **View Job** screen, clicking **\[Edit\]** will take you to a screen where you can modify the job details.

![Editing Job](images/editing-job.png){width=5.16in}

- Job Code**: Displayed as read-only and** cannot be edited**.**

- Job Title**: Editable in both** English (EN) **and** French (FR)**.**

- Description**: Editable field to update the job description.**

- Status**:**

  - Active**: The job is active and will be available in the** Job selection dropdown **on the** Dealer Employee Management **screen.**

  - Inactive**: The job is inactive and will** not be available **as a selection option on the Dealer Employee Management screen.**

**Menu Permission & Quick Links**

- The **menu permissions** **and Quick Links** appear the same as when the job was originally created.\
  You can review and modify them if needed.

**Action Buttons**

- **\[Cancel\]**: Cancels the action and returns to the previous screen.

- **\[Save\]**: Becomes active only when changes are made. Saves and applies the modifications.

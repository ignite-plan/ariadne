**Login method**

- Go to https://dealers.kia-corp.io/ca/ to access the first screen.
- You will be directed to the HMG partner login screen.
- Select Personal Login. 
- Log in using your HMA Partner ID and password.


![kia login](images/image-21.png){width=4.2in}

- When accessing Dealers for the first time, you will be asked to agree to the HMG Partner ID service terms.
- Atfer that, you will be directed to the Dealer service terms and personal information consent screen.

![Service terms](images/image-22.png){width=6.27in}


**Logout method**

- User logout instructions:
  - Click on the profile image area at the top left of the screen.
  - An Account sheet will appear.
  - Select Logout from this sheet.


- **Automatic logout instructions**:
  - In accordance with security policy, Dealers will automatically log you out if no activity is detected on the site for 60 minutes.
  - After 55 minutes of inactivity, a popup will appear notifying you that logout will occur in 5 minutes.
  - If inactivity is detected for 60 minutes, automatic logout will be initiated.
  - If a user returns to the Dealers screen after 60 minutes have passed, they will be automatically logged out.


![Session end pop-up](images/image-27.png){width=6.27in}

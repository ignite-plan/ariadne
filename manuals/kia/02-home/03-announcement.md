## Announcement

- This is an area where bulletin boards registered as announcements among hmg admin \> Dealers bulletin boards are displayed, and a maximum of 10 posts are shown.

- Posts are displayed in order of most recently registered important notice posts, followed by most recently registered general posts.

![Announcement](images/announcement.png){width=6.79in}

- **Important icon:** If a post is set as a pinned/important post, it is fixed at the top of the list during the configured period.

- **Title:** When selected, you will be directed to the detailed screen of that post.

- **Date:** The date when the post was published is displayed.

- **New flag**: The **New** flag is automatically applied to new posts according to the tenant configuration.

![Announcement -- Icon/Title/Date/Flag](images/announcement-icon-title-date-flag.png){width=3.71in}

- **\[More\]:** When clicked, you will be directed to the Announcement bulletin board screen, taking you to the page where you can view the entire list of Announcement posts.

**View Announcement Post List**

![Announcement List](images/announcement-list.png){width=5.75in}

- The post list screen consists of a **title, search area, and search results** display area.

- **Search filter:** You can search for posts according to specific conditions in the search area.

- Date:

![Announcement - Search Filter](images/announcement-search-filter.png){width=6.40in}

- You can select **Title(default), key word,** or **Post ID** fields to search for posts that match the search criteria.

![](images/announcement-img-1.png){width=3.61in}

**Search filter - Search criteria**

- **The post search area** shows the total number of posts, and the list displays Title, Attachment, and Date information.

- Clicking on the Attachment filename allows you to **preview** it. **(For Attachment preview details, refer to 4. Bulletin board \> Attachment area)**

- **\[reset\]:** When clicked, resets the filter conditions and search results.

- **\[search\]:** When clicked, displays the search results for posts that match the set conditions.

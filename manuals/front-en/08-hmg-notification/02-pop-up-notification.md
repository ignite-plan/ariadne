## Pop-up Notification

- HMG Pop-up notifications registered in HMG-notice are displayed.

- They can be displayed based on buttons added in HMG-notice, and clicking the **button allows** navigation to specific pages.

- It is possible to set **pop-up notifications** to be displayed for a **specific period** (3 days, 7 days), and it is also possible to set them to always be displayed.

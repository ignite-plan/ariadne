## Bulletin Highlight

- The post collection feature is an area where posts registered as **Important notices** in general bulletin boards are collected and displayed, and only posts from bulletin boards with permissions are shown.

- A maximum of 10 posts are sorted by the most recent registration order.

- Title and date information are displayed.

![Bulletin Highlights on Home Screen](images/bulletin-highlights-on-home-screen.png){width=4.58in}

- **Title:** When selected, you will be directed to the detailed screen of that post.

- **Date:** The date when the post was published is displayed.

- **New flag**: The **New** flag is automatically applied to new posts according to the tenant configuration.

![Bulletin Highlight -- Icon/Title/Date/Flag](images/bulletin-highlight-icon-title-date-flag.png){width=4.22in}

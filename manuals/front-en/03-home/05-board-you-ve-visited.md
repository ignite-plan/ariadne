## Board you've visited

- This is a feature that allows users to quickly access **recently visited** menus.

- The list displays up to five.

- Clicking on a registered menu in the list will take you to that menu screen.

- Pressing the **star icon** registers or removes it from the Quick-Links list above.

- If 20 Quick-Links are already registered, new ones cannot be added.

- Hovering over a menu name displays the full menu path.

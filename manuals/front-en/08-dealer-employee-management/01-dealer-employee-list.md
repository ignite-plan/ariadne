## Dealer employee List

- This is a screen where you can view dealer employee information.

- **Clicking \[create new\]** at the top will take you to a screen to create new employee information.

![Employee List](images/employee-list.png){width=6.79in}

- You can view the dealer employee list according to employee conditions.

![Employee List - Search Filter](images/employee-list-search-filter.png){width=6.79in}

- **User Name:** You can search by the user\'s name.

- **User No.:** You can search by the user\'s User No.

- **Partner id:** You can search by the user\'s HMG Partner id.

- **Job title:** Select the job title assigned to the user. You can select more than one, and the selected items appear in the job title area below.

- **Status:** You can view according to the status of the user account.

- **Active:** Account activated status

- **Inactive:** Account deactivated status

- Clicking the **\[search\]** button displays the results of the set conditions.

- Clicking the **\[reset\] icon** button resets everything.

<!-- -->

- The dealer employee list provides the following information:

  - **User No.:** A unique user number generated by the Dealers system.

  - **Partner ID:** The user\'s Partner ID.

  - **Name:** Provides first name and last name combined

  - If designated as the dealer principal of the dealership, a badge is displayed in the list.\
    ![](images/dealer-employee-list-img-1.png){width=0.28in}

  - **Job title:** The user\'s permission

  - **Updated date:** The date when the user\'s account was created or modified

  - **Status:** The status of the account

  - Clicking on the Partner ID or name area allows you to check the detailed information of that user.

## Bulletin Board Search & Result

- You can search by **Board ID** or **Board Name** in the **search bar**.\
  As you type, **real-time keyword search** is performed.

- **Search Behavior & Conditions**

<!-- -->

- Keywords are searched in **real time** as you type.

- Matching keywords are **highlighted** in the search results.

- Clicking the **\[X\]** button on the right will **clear the input**, resetting the search to its default state.

- If no results are found, a **"No search results"** message will be displayed.

<!-- -->

- The bulletin board name and description are displayed at the top.

- A search area and post list for viewing posts belonging to that bulletin board are displayed

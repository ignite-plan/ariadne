- The **Post Management** page in the **BO** allows users to **create**, **edit**, and **delete** posts under each bulletin board.

- When clicking on a **Bulletin Board**, you can check whether it is **linked to a menu**.\
  The attributes of each post are determined based on the **Post Attribute Settings** of the selected bulletin board.

- When you access the Post Management menu \> search for bulletin board name/bulletin board ID, select a bulletin board, and click Search, you can view the bulletin board.

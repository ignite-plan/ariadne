## List Setting View

- The **List Setting** function allows you to manage the **fields** and their **display order** on the front-end bulletin board.

- The **Order**, **Field Name**, and **Display Setting** shown in the List Setting are based on the attributes configured in the **Bulletin Board Details** tab.

![Bulletin Board -- List setting tab](images/bulletin-board-list-setting-tab.png){width=4.99in}

- **Order**: Indicates the **display order** of each **Field Name** in the front-end bulletin board list.

- **Field Name**: The name displayed in the front-end list. This is composed of:

- **Default fields** (e.g., Post ID, Title, Date)

  - **Additional attributes** where **Usage Status = ON**\
    If the field is marked as **required**, an asterisk (*) will be shown next to the field name. A field is considered required under the following conditions:

  - It is a **default field** (Post ID, Title, Date

  - The **Required Field** option is set to **ON** in the Additional Attributes.

  - **Display Setting**: Indicates whether the field is visible in the front-end list.

    - **Y**: The field is **visible**

    - **N**: The field is **hidden**

- **\[Edit\]**: Clicking this button opens the **List Setting Edit** screen.

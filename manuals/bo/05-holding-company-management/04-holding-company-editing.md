## Holding Company Editing

![Editing Holding Company](images/editing-holding-company.png){width=6.27in}

- This screen allows modification of holding company information.

**Holding company information**

- **Company name**: Cannot be edited.

- **Name of company representative**: Can be edited.

**Affiliate Dealer List**

- The Available Dealer list includes dealers eligible for addition and dealers already affiliated.

- Dealer name, number, and status are shown.

  - Dealers are displayed in the following status priority: Active, Under Termination, Inactive, Terminated then sorted by name.

- Search dealers by name in both the Available Dealer list and Selected dealers.

- Checked dealers are added to Selected dealers.

- Unchecking or clicking the x removes them from Selected dealers.

**Action Buttons**

- **\[cancel\]**: Discards any changes and returns to the previous screen.

- **\[save\]**: Triggers a popup displaying the names and numbers of dealers added or removed.

![Change Save Popup](images/change-save-popup-2.png){width=3.83in}

- Click **\[confirm\]** to apply the changes.

- Click **\[Cancel\]** in the popup to discard the changes and return to the edit screen.

- Clicking the **\[cancel\]** button on the main screen will also discard all changes and navigate back.

## Holding Company List View

![Holding Company List](images/holding-company-list.png){width=6.27in}

- This screen allows you to create, edit, delete, and view information about holding companies.

- You can view the list of registered holding companies.

  - **No**: Internal holding company number managed by the dealer system.

  - **Holding company name**: Name of the holding company.

  - **Company representative**: Name of the holding company's representative.

  - **Dealers**: Number of dealers assigned to the holding company.

  - **Updated by**: ID of the user who created or last modified the holding company.

  - **Updated date**: Date when the holding company was created or last updated.

  <!-- -->

  - **Detail**: Navigates to the detail view of the holding company.

- Click the **\[create new\]** button to move to the creation screen for a new holding company.

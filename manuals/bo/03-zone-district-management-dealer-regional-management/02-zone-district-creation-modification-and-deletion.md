## Zone/District Creation, Modification, and Deletion

![Zone on Hover](images/zone-on-hover.png){width=3.92in}

- Click the **\[add zone\]** button at the top to add a Zone in the box below.

Zone names must be unique and cannot be duplicated.

Hovering over a Zone will display a **+** button. Clicking this will create a new District under the selected Zone.

District names must also be unique within the same Zone.

Hovering over a Zone will display a **pencil icon**. Click this icon to edit the name.

Hovering over a Zone will also display a **--** button. Clicking this will delete the Zone.

Deleting a Zone will also delete all its associated Districts.

Hovering over a District will display a **--** button. Clicking this will delete the District.

If a District is deleted, the dealers previously assigned to that District will retain the Zone assignment but will no longer belong to any District.

The order of Zones and Districts can be rearranged.

Click and hold the item you want to move for 1 second to activate drag-and-drop.

When a Zone is moved, its sub-Districts will move together.

Districts can only be moved within the same Zone.

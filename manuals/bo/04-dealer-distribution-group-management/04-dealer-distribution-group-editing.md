## Dealer Distribution Group Editing

![Editing Group](images/editing-group.png){width=5.27in}

- You can edit the dealer group name, description, and assigned dealers.

- **Dealer group information**

<!-- -->

- Dealer group name: Can be edited.

- Description: Can be edited.

**Dealer list**

- Displays the full list of available dealers.

- Dealers already assigned to the group are pre-selected.

- Shows dealer name, number, and status.

  - Status display order: Active, Under Termination, Inactive, Terminated then sorted alphabetically.

- Dealers can be searched by name.

- Checked dealers are added to Selected dealers.

  - Unchecking or pressing x removes them from Selected dealers.

- If there are changes in the dealer list and you click the **\[save\]** button, a popup will appear.

![Change Save Popup](images/change-save-popup.png){width=2.76in}

- The popup shows the names and numbers of dealers added or removed.

- Click **\[confirm\]** to apply the changes.

- Click **\[cancel\]** to discard the changes and return to the edit screen.

<!-- -->

- Pressing the **\[cancel\]** button discards any changes and returns to the previous screen.
